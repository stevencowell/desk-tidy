# Desk Tidy guided course source notes

## Authoritative project sources

- `Stage 4 Technology Mandatory - desk tidy (1).pdf` supplied the ten-week project sequence, the 200 x 20 x 10 mm practice slat, named materials and joints, the 80-120-240 grit sanding sequence, one clear water-based varnish coat and specified functional-test items.
- `NESA - technology_7_8_2023 (S4).docx` and the syllabus support document supplied the Stage 4 outcome wording shown in `teacher-resources.html`; the local wording was reverified against the live NESA source on 22 August 2026.
- The Wagga Wagga High School Year 8 Assessment Handbook 2026, Mandatory Technology schedule on page 14, is the latest verified school assessment authority found. It schedules Project and Practical at 60%, Folio at 25% and Design Knowledge at 15%, due at the end of Semesters 1 and 2.
- The original `desk tidy picture.png` is retained and reused as `assets/desk-tidy-reference.png`.
- Relevant existing workshop photographs and new source-safe teaching diagrams are used only where they support the named theory content. Generated diagrams do not supply project dimensions, tolerances or a construction plan.

## Authority boundary

- The current school handbook overrides the older 60/20/20 weighting printed in the project unit for current planning claims.
- The site is prepared for 2027 delivery. The 2027 schedule, formal task notifications, exact dates, criteria, conditions, adjustments and submission method remain `Teacher to confirm`.
- No approved dimensioned construction drawing, component schedule, finished dimensions, tolerances or fixed joint plan was supplied. The reference image is not a fabrication plan.
- Current teacher direction, induction, OnGuard, SOP, SWMS, SDS, product information and school workshop controls govern practical work.

## New authored content

- The fifteen theory sections were drafted in ChatGPT, one named section at a time, then checked and integrated into five two-week modules.
- The twelve numbered folio prompts form one consistent evidence sequence with stable anchors, browser autosave and student-controlled backup.
- Knowledge checks, written scaffolds, feedback and model responses were written specifically for the Desk Tidy project and its authorised sequence.
- Fifteen applied activities and their printable alternatives were built specifically for the fifteen named theory sections.
- Video learning is matched section by section. Where a safe direct match is unavailable, the course presents an honest on-site alternative instead of using a misleading clip.

## Student evidence behaviour

- Each module autosaves independently in the current browser profile and device.
- The folio autosaves separately and supports JSON or ZIP backup and restore.
- Browser storage is not a submission system. Students should save module evidence as PDF and use the teacher-provided submission method.

## Publication target

The canonical release target is `https://stevencowell.github.io/desk-tidy/`. A successful Git push is not treated as deployment proof; the rendered GitHub Pages site must be checked separately after publication.
