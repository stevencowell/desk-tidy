Course context

Outcomes and assessment from the project unit

Assessment weighting

Practical project - 60%
Safe work habits and OnGuard safety quiz - 20%
Design folio, working drawings and evaluation - 20%

The guided pages build theory understanding and editable evidence. Practical performance and teacher judgement remain separate.

Syllabus coverage

The unit supports all six Stage 4 Materials and Production Processes outcomes through design, practical work, evidence and evaluation.

View the six syllabus outcomes
  1. TE4-SDP-01: explains relationships between sustainability, design and production.
  2. TE4-PDP-01: describes the practices and processes of designers and producers.
  3. TE4-MSC-01: explains how materials, systems and components contribute to solutions.
  4. TE4-DES-01: communicates and evaluates design ideas and solutions.
  5. TE4-PPM-01: applies processes in the planning, management and production of projects.
  6. TE4-SAF-01: selects and safely uses tools, materials, technologies and processes.

Teacher-controlled assessment

Stage 4 End-of-Year Class Test

An informal, unweighted, password-protected 40-mark class test covering the complete Desk Tidy pathway. The teacher will confirm the date. Responses autosave independently on this device and can be printed or saved as a PDF for submission.
