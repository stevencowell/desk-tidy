How the course works

One predictable routine for every two-week module

Build understanding before answering, then use feedback and appropriate response examples to improve your evidence.

1. Read and inspect

Study the project-specific theory, photographs, diagrams and workshop examples.

2. Practise

3. Apply

4. Review and save
