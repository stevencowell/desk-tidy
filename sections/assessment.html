Assessment context

The Desk Tidy project, folio and safety work support Stage 4 learning. The current Industrial Arts handbook tables reviewed for this package do not schedule Desk Tidy as a separate formal weighted task, so this page does not invent a task number, weighting or due date.

  • Project and folio: classwork evidence; local requirements and due date are Teacher to confirm.
  • Safe workshop practice: ongoing teacher observation and feedback.
  • End-of-year class test: informal practice/classwork, unweighted; date Teacher to confirm.
