Evidence, feedback and support

EvidenceLook forRespond / support
Website theory and checksNamed section completed; quiz responses show understandingReturn to missed items; use the scaffolded response before rechecking
Folio and planningClear decisions, drawings and manageable production stepsConference against the brief; set one specific next action
Practical observationSafety, accuracy, workflow, quality and clean-downPause, demonstrate, practise on off-cuts and recheck
Product and evaluationFunctional tests and evidence-based judgementCompare the result directly with the original criteria

Differentiation and adjustments: use visual steps, demonstrations, word banks, chunked instructions, accessible practical roles, additional processing time and approved ways to record evidence without reducing the intended outcome.

Literacy: explicitly teach the design vocabulary, model evidence-based explanations and use sentence scaffolds before independent responses. Numeracy: teach millimetres, 1:10 representation, datum-based measurement, quantities, sequence and time planning through approved student drawings rather than invented project dimensions. Digital literacy: teach source attribution, safe browser use, local-save limits, backup and restore as general learning processes; this does not add a Digital/communication outcome to the Desk Tidy unit.

Practical controls: the project unit controls the sequence; current school induction, PPE, tool approvals, supervision, risk controls, SDS and product instructions control delivery.
