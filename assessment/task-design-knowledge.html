Published outcome focus

TE4-SDP-01, TE4-PDP-01, TE4-MSC-01, TE4-DIG-01 and/or TE4-DIG-02, plus content drawn from the relevant focus area.

These codes reproduce the 2026 whole-subject schedule. They do not add Digital outcomes to the Desk Tidy unit mapping.

Use this course to prepare

  • Read all fifteen named theory sections across the five modules.
  • Complete each group of ten checks and use the specific feedback to correct errors.
  • Write the scaffolded responses before viewing the appropriate response examples.
  • Use applied activities to explain decisions, diagnose faults and connect evidence with design criteria.
  • Save the module evidence PDFs before changing devices.

The 40-mark digital class test is available for practice only. It is not labelled as the formal Design Knowledge task.

Knowledge is more than recall

Be ready to explain relationships: why a control is stronger, why a material property matters, why a drawing or datum prevents error, why a joint suits a component relationship, and what test evidence supports an evaluation.
