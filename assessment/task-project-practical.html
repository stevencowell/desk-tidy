Project and Practical

Use the course to collect clear evidence of an approved design, safe production, quality decisions and functional testing.

Authority

The verified 2026 Year 8 handbook schedules Project and Practical at 60%, due at the end of Semesters 1 and 2. This is a subject-level schedule, not an authority to invent a Desk Tidy-specific mark allocation.

Teacher to confirm for 2027: whether and how this Desk Tidy project contributes, the issued task number, mark value, dates, criteria, conditions, adjustments and submission method.

Published outcome focus

TE4-DES-01, TE4-PPM-01, TE4-SAF-01 and TE4-MSC-01, plus content drawn from the relevant focus area.

Evidence this course helps you prepare

  • An approved design brief, criteria and selected concept.
  • Teacher-approved drawings, cutting list and production schedule. No dimensions should be guessed from the reference image.
  • Safe-work evidence based on current teacher direction, induction, OnGuard, SOP, SWMS, SDS and product information where required.
  • Process photographs and short notes showing accurate marking, cutting, joint preparation, dry fitting, assembly, sanding and finishing.
  • Functional tests using the intended stationery, plus honest evidence of stability, access, desk fit and workmanship.

Teacher approval required before production

Before you submit anything

  1. Read the formal notification issued by your teacher.
  2. Check the exact due date, task conditions and required files or physical items.
  3. Make sure every photograph and statement is your own authentic evidence.
  4. Use the teacher-provided submission method; browser storage is not a submission system.
